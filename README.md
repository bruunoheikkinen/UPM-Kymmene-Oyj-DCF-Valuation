# UPM-Kymmene Oyj DCF Valuation & Financial Model

## Overview

A DCF valuation and integrated financial model of UPM-Kymmene Oyj, incorporating segment-level operating forecasts, macroeconomic drivers, and three-statement financial modeling.

## Methodology

- Historical financial analysis (2023-2025) and forecasts through 2030
- Segment-level revenue, EBIT margin, D&A, and cost forecasts across UPM’s business areas
- Macro-linked sensitivities for pulp prices, EUR/USD, Nordic electricity prices, and EU construction activity
- Integrated income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement
- Supporting working capital, asset, debt, and equity schedules
- DCF valuation using perpetuity-growth and EV/EBITDA exit-multiple methods
- WACC, terminal-value, and exit-multiple sensitivity analysis

## Key Features

The model applies bear, base, and bull operating cases by segment, with selected business areas linked to relevant macroeconomic drivers. Forecasts feed into an integrated three-statement model and unlevered free cash flow valuation, with balance sheet and cash flow reconciliation checks.
